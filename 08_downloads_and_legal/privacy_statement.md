# Privacy Policy Statement

NPA is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe online experience on our website. This Statement of Privacy applies to the NPA website and governs data collection and usage. By using the NPA website, users consent to the data practices described in this statement.

**DATA COLLECTION:**

NPA collects information directly provided by Users. For example, we collect information when the User creates an account, subscribes, participates in any interactive feature of our services, fill out a form, request customer support or otherwise communicate with us. The type of information we may collect include name, email address, phone number, work/home address, credit card information and other contact or identifying information as maybe provided.

We collect anonymous data from every visitor of the website to monitor traffic, improve our service and fix bugs. For example, we collect information like web requests, the data sent in response to such requests, the Internet Protocol (IP) address, the computer hardware and software, the browser type, the browser language, and a timestamp for the request.

We also use various technologies to collect information and may include sending cookies to Users’ computer. Cookies are small data files stored on a hard drive or in a device memory that helps us improve our services and Users experience, see which areas and features of our services are popular and count visits. This information is then used for targeted or personalized content for the purpose of improving web browsing experience on our website. Cookies are files you can delete.

**USE OF THE DATA**

NPA will only use personal information obtained from Users to provide requested services or to communicate with the User about our products and services.

NPA will not use or disclose sensitive personal information, such as race, religion, or political affiliations without consent.

In addition, NPA may share data with trusted partners to help us perform statistical analysis, send email or postal mail, provide customer support, or arrange for deliveries. All such third parties are prohibited from using personal information obtained from Users except to provide these services to NPA and are required to maintain the confidentiality of such information.

NPA may track the pages our customers visit within its website in order to determine which services are the most popular. This data is used to deliver targeted content to customers whose behaviour indicates that they are interested in a particular subject area.

We employ industry standard techniques to protect against unauthorized access to data about Users that we store, including personal information.

NPA will not share personal information provided to us without Users’ consent.

**SECURITY**

NPA takes reasonable steps to protect personally identifiable information from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. NPA secures Users personal information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure. We ensure that personally identifiable information provided on computer servers is stored in a controlled, secure environment, protected from unauthorized access, use or disclosure. When personal information (such as a credit card number) is transmitted to other websites, it is protected through the use of encryption, such as the Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocol.

This Privacy Policy may be amended from time to time to reflect any amendment or change in policy. Use of information we collect now is subject to the Privacy Policy in effect at the time such information is used.

We welcome any comments and questions regarding this Statement of Privacy. Please write us at info@nigerianports.gov.ng.
