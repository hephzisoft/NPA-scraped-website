**_REGISTRATION AS CLEARING AND FORWARDING AGENTS_**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director with the following documents:

  * Certificate of Company Registration
  * Memorandum and Article of Association
  * Bank Reference
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate
  * Evidence of Registration with Council for the Regulation of Freight Forwarding in Nigeria (CRFFN)

Registration fee: ₦200,000.00 payable to NPA revenue account.

Certificate is valid for One (1) year.

Annual Renewal Fee: ₦100,000.00

Timeline: 6 weeks
