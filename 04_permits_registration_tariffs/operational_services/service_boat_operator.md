**_REGISTRATION AS A SERVICE BOAT OPERATOR_**

Letter of Application to GM (Eastern or Western Ports) with the following documents:

  * Certificate of Company Registration
  * NPA Shipping Company Registration Certificate
  * Memorandum and Article of Association
  * Bank Reference
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate

Registration Fee: ₦200,000.00 payable to NPA revenue account.

Timeline: 6 weeks
