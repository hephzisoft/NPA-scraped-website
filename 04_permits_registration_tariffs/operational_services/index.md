OPERATIONAL SERVICES/FACILITIES – Requirements for Registration and Certification

#### __REGISTRATION AS A SHIPPING COMPANY/AGENT

**_REGISTRATION AS A SHIPPING COMPANY/AGENT_**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director, with the following accompanying documents:

  * Certificate of Company Registration
  * Memorandum and Article of Association
  * Bank Reference
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate

Registration Fee: N230,000.00 payable to NPA revenue account.

Certificate is valid for One (1) year.

Annual Renewal Fee: ₦115,000.00

Timeline: 10 Working Days

#### __REGISTRATION AS A SERVICE BOAT OPERATOR

**_REGISTRATION AS A SERVICE BOAT OPERATOR_**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director, with the following accompanying documents:

  * Certification of Company Registration
  * NPA Shipping Company Registration Certificate
  * Memorandum and Article of Association
  * NPA register Service Boat Agent appointed by any Service Boat Operator
  * Bank Reference
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate
  * Registration Fee N115,000.00 payable to NPA revenue account

**_Timeline:_ **72 Hours

#### __REGISTRATION AS CLEARING AND FORWARDING AGENTS

**_REGISTRATION AS CLEARING AND FORWARDING AGENTS_**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director, with the following documents:

  * Certificate of Company Registration
  * Evidence of Registration with Nigeria Customs Service
  * Memorandum and Article of Association
  * Bank Reference
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate

Registration fee: N28,750.00 payable to NPA revenue account.

Certificate is valid for One (1) year.

Annual Renewal Fee: ₦5,750.00

Timeline: 10 Working Days

#### __REGISTRATION AS CHANDLERS

**_REGISTRATION AS CHANDLERS_**

Letter of Application to the Port Manager with the following accompanying documents:

  * Certificate of Registration with CAC
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate
  * Customs approval with Form C1

Registration fee:₦57,500.00 payable to NPA revenue account (exclusive of 7.5% VAT and ₦805 Documentation Fee).

Certificate is valid for One (1) year.

Renewal Fee: ₦34,500.00

Timeline: 3 Days (subject to submission of complete documents)

#### __BONDED TERMINALS

**_BONDED TERMINALS_**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director Nigerian Ports Authority with the following accompanying documents:

  * Company Registration with CAC
  * Three years Tax Clearance Certificate
  * Tax Identification Number (TIN)
  * Insurance Cover.

Timeline: 10 Days

#### __PILOTAGE EXEMPTION CERTIFICATE

**_PILOTAGE EXEMPTION CERTIFICATE_**

  * Evidence of passing exam conducted by the District pilotage Board.
  * Satisfactory Medical certificate from a qualified practitioner recognized by NPA.
  * Satisfactory knowledge of international regulations for preventing collisions at sea, local regulations, NPA bye-laws, local Notice to Mariners and marine emergency procedures in force.
  * Visit to the operation room of NPA port control within the preceding two months prior to the examination.
  * Satisfactory working knowledge of English language.
  * Knowledge of dangerous substances/objects in the harbour area.
  * The certificate shall bear the description and class of vessel the holder is authorized to pilot.
  * The certificate is non-transferable.
  * The Certificate holder shall conform strictly to all pilotage requirements.
  * Certificate is renewable every year.

Pilotage Exemption fee: ₦46,000.00/Pilot/Vessel/Pilotage District payable to NPA revenue account.

Timeline: 10 Working Days
