#### __Operational Requirements of a Jetty  
  
  * Proof of holding bay inside and outside the Jetty
  * Installation of flow meters
  * Provision of office accommodation for government agencies
  * Provision of Ship profile/Digital Board

#### __Technical Requirements of a Jetty

  * Provision of Bathymetric charts for location and approach
  * Architectural Drawings
  * Structural Drawing/Details
  * Structural Design Calculation
  * Bollards/dolphins arrangement and load test
  * Material Schedule
  * Geotechnical investigation
  * Proof of ownership of the Land
  * Transportation Impact Assessment (TIA)
  * Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

#### __Safety and Security Requirements of a Jetty

  * Proof of Port Facility Security Assessment (PFSA) and Port Facility Security Plan (PFSP)
  * Engagement of a certified Port Facility Security Officer (PFSO)
  * Provision of the following security equipment: 
    * High security perimeter fence equivalent to BSI 1722 part 10
    * Access control equipment
    * Closed Circuit Television (CCTV)
    * Modern gate fitted with speed barriers
    * Proper illumination
  * Emergency access and exit routes
  * Availability of pollution control equipment and operating personnel
  * Provision of life saving aids
  * Provision of handrails on the deck of the jetty
  * Provision of firefighting equipment
  * Proof of implementation of approved safety plan
  * Proof of implementation of approved HSE Policy
  * All vessels berthed must ensure that a safe means of access is provided.
  * No double banking of vessels would be granted.
  * Master of vessel must ensure that this access remains safe at all times while alongside and to adjust gangway should the situation becomes unsafe.

#### __Bunkering Services Requirement

  1. Requests for bunker should be directed to Port Managers. Approvals for bunker should be restricted to office hours only (0800-1700hrs).
  2. Bunker suppliers/chandlers shall apply/notify NPA directly and seek approval before commencement of bunkering.
  3. Bunker suppliers shall provide the following information: 
     * Name of Bunker Supplier.
     * Name of Bunker Barge (or truck number)
     * Quantity to be supplied.
     * Location of supply.
     * Date and time of supply.
     * Receiving vessel.
