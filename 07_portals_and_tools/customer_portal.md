# NPA Customer Portal

- **Official URL:** [https://customers.nigerianports.gov.ng/](https://customers.nigerianports.gov.ng/)
- **Classification:** External Digital Portal / E-Services System

## Overview
The official online billing, invoicing, tracking, and customer services management portal for port users, shipping companies, freight forwarders, and terminal operators.

## Core Capabilities & Features
- Online customer registration and verification
- Electronic billing and payment receipt generation
- Vessel and container clearance status tracking
- Direct helpdesk and ticket tracking

## Technical Details
- **Hosting / Subdomain:** `https://customers.nigerianports.gov.ng/`
- **Security:** HTTPS secured with authenticated access controls.
- **Support & Inquiries:** Contact NPA Headquarters / Port Operation centers for portal authorization.
