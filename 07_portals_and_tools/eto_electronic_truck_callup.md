# Eto Electronic Truck Call-up System

- **Official URL:** [https://eto.ttp.ng/](https://eto.ttp.ng/)
- **Classification:** External Digital Portal / E-Services System

## Overview
The digital call-up system operated in partnership with Trucks Transit Parks (TTP) to schedule, regulate, and control truck transit into Lagos Port Complex and Tin Can Island Port.

## Core Capabilities & Features
- Driver and truck electronic booking
- Designated pre-gate and satellite truck park tracking
- Time-slotted electronic entry permits
- Traffic congestion mitigation along the port access corridors

## Technical Details
- **Hosting / Subdomain:** `https://eto.ttp.ng/`
- **Security:** HTTPS secured with authenticated access controls.
- **Support & Inquiries:** Contact NPA Headquarters / Port Operation centers for portal authorization.
