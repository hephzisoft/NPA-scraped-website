**ISSUANCE OF PERMIT FOR PIPELINE LAYING AND RE-LAYING**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director with the following accompanying documents:

  1. Survey plan / bathymetric chart of the proposed location of the proposed pipeline route(s).
  2. Details of the pipeline route(s), location, coordinates, width and length, Start and end points.
  3. Date and duration of the proposed activity.
  4. Location of the area of proposed activity in relation to the immediate surrounding land and channel for navigation

Processing fee: N1,500,000.00 payable to NPA Revenue Account

The Permit is valid for One (1) fiscal year

Annual Renewal is N1,500,000.00

Timeline: Six (6) weeks

  * Dredging/Sandwinning/Sandfilling

**ISSUANCE OF PERMIT FOR DREDGING/SANDWINNING/SANDFILLING**

Letter of Application to the Managing Director with the following accompanying documents:

  1. Bathymetric chart of the proposed area to be dredged showing name, coordinates of the location and proximity to surrounding land and channel for navigation
  2. Depth to be achieved during the dredging.
  3. Location and coordinates of proposed dumpsite
  4. Exact volume and type of material to be dredged
  5. Date and duration of the proposed activity

**** Processing fee: N1,500,000.00 payable to NPA Revenue Account

The Permit is valid for One (1) fiscal year

Annual Renewal is N1,500,000.00

Timeline: Six (6) weeks
