##### **Friday, August 16 th 2024**

**NPA Management Appoints New Port Managers**

The Managing Director of Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA) Dr. Abubakar Dantsoho has approved the appointment of 6 new port managers.

In a recent disposition signed by the Managing Director, Mr. Adebowale Lawal Ibrahim is to serve as Port Manager, Lagos Port Complex while Mr. Abubakar Sani Isa is the Port Manager, Tin Can Island Port.

Also appointed are Mr. Abdulrahman Hussaini, for Onne Port, Mr. Sa’adu Dahiru Mohammed, Delta Ports Warri, Mr. Ekine Ibifiri Alex for Calabar Port, and Mr. Emmanuel Anda has been posted to Lekki Port as Port Manager.

The Port Manager of Rivers Port, Mrs Kenechi Edith Okezie has been retained.

The new appointments are with immediate effect.****
